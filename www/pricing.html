How many seats do you need?

Most companies charge less per seat as you add seats. We believe collaboration is a premium feature, so each seat costs more than the last. Economies of scale, in reverse.

Seat n is priced at $1,249 × 1.4n−1/mo, a formula our pricing team describes as "brave". Prices exclude tax, gratuity, and per-pixel rendering fees. A seat is defined as any chair within line of sight of a screen displaying jSuite. Standing users are billed as 0.8 seats, rounded up to 1.

Frequently asked questions

Asked frequently, answered reluctantly.

Why does this cost money? It all runs on my own machine.

Yes. You are paying for the numbers on this page. The numbers are premium numbers.

What payment methods do you accept?

None. There is no payment processor, no backend, and no company. The upgrade button scrolls you to a slider. This has not stopped anyone from asking about purchase orders.

Why does each additional seat cost more?

Every seat you add proves jSuite is more valuable to your organization. We simply price in that enthusiasm. Our competitors call this "backwards"; we call it "value-based pricing".
